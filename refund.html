Refund policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Every piece is made by hand just for you, so here's exactly when money comes back, with no surprises.

Your deposit

  • Before I start cutting or printing: cancel and I refund your full deposit.
  • Once work has started: the deposit can't be refunded, because the materials and hours are already spent on your piece.
  • If I'm more than 7 days late: you can cancel and get your full deposit back, even if work has started.

When your piece is finished

  • You see photos of your finished sword before you pay the rest.
  • If anything doesn't match the design you approved, I fix it for free. Small touch-ups are free too.
  • After you approve the finished piece there are no refunds. A full repaint or a design change after approval is paid.

Damage in shipping

Send me photos or your unboxing video within 24 hours of delivery (counted from the courier's tracking record) and I'll repair or replace it. Damage from impact or misuse after delivery isn't covered.

Rush orders

If a paid rush order is late, I refund the rush fee.

How refunds are paid

By InstaPay, back to the account that paid, within 7 days of us agreeing the refund.

How to ask

Use the “Email us” button at the bottom of the page, or message me on Instagram @guts.decor, with your order code.
