Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

I'm Meyer, and I run Guts Decor from Cairo, Egypt. This page explains, in plain words, what I collect when you order, why, and what you can ask me to do with it. I collect as little as possible.

What I collect

  • From the order form: your name, email, phone number, delivery address, the sword and options you choose, engraving text, notes, and, if you fill them in, a gift recipient's name, an occasion date, a friend's code and how you found me.
  • In the Instagram chat: the payment receipts you send me there. They stay in that chat.
  • I don't collect card numbers, ID numbers or passwords, and the site has no accounts or logins.

Why I collect it

  • To make your order, confirm your date and price, and email you about it.
  • To deliver it: the courier gets your name, phone number and address, nothing else.
  • To check your payment, and to honour my guarantee if anything goes wrong.

I never sell your data, and I never use it for anything other than your order.

Where it's kept and who sees it

Your order details are stored privately in my own secure account, and only I can see them. Payment receipts you send me in the Instagram chat stay in that chat. Nothing about you is stored on the website itself.

How long I keep it

I keep your order details for up to 2 years after delivery, so I can honour the guarantee and keep basic business records. After that I delete them. You can ask me to delete them sooner at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data. Use the “Email us” button at the bottom of any page, or message me on Instagram @guts.decor, and include your order code if you have one. I'll reply within 7 days. I handle personal data in line with Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 151 of 2020).

Outside services this site uses

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. Like any web host, it may log basic technical details such as your IP address for security.
  • Google securely receives and stores your order, and sends my order emails.
  • Google Fonts provides the lettering, so your browser connects to Google to load it.
  • YouTube plays my talking videos in privacy-enhanced mode. Nothing loads from YouTube until you press play (only the cover picture may come from YouTube's image server).
  • Instagram links open Instagram, which has its own privacy policy.

Your cart and one reminder

Your cart is kept in your own browser, not on my side. If you type your email at checkout but don't finish, I keep only that email and your cart, and send you one reminder after 24 hours. Then it's deleted, after 7 days at most, or as soon as you order.

Cookies and counting

This site uses no tracking cookies, no analytics service and no ad pixels, so there's no cookie banner. It only counts visits and button taps anonymously (for example "the Gifts page was opened"), with nothing about who you are. Your browser remembers your language choice and your cart on your own device.

Age

This site and my pieces are for adults (18+).

Changes

If I change this policy, I'll update the date at the top of this page.
